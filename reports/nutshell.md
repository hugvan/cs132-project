# Nutshell

**Headline** (7 words)

eFOI Response Times and Request Outcomes, 2025

**Caption** (103 words)

Response times and observed outcomes for 1,641 sampled public eFOI requests filed in 2025. Each equal-sized point represents one request. Horizontal positions indicate elapsed time to the first agency reply on a logarithmic scale; vertical offsets prevent overlap and have no numerical meaning. Requests without an agency reply occupy a separate categorical column. Blue includes successful and partially successful requests; pink indicates unsuccessful requests; gray includes open and referred requests. The sample is stratified by agency group and filing quarter; points are unweighted and do not represent population proportions. Statuses were observed on 29 September 2026. A first reply may be an acknowledgment.
