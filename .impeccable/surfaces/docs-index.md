## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Structure the 2025 Philippine eFOI research portfolio as an ink-and-paper public records audit dossier where visitors pull an interactive agency queue ticket and trace a scroll-drawn paper trail to discover why *"a fast reply is not a yes."* Refuses the warm-cream/forest-green marketing template and collapsible-hidden methodology gates.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Bond-paper white (`#fafaf8`) and ledger tint (`#f2f2ee`), audit ink deep indigo (`#1c1246`), 1px hairline ledger rules (`#d2d4cf`), rectangular rubber-stamp signal inks (crimson alert `#a62626`, cobalt verification `#1d4ed8`, deep slate `#334155`), lining tabular numerals (`"tnum" 1, "onum" 0`), and a darkroom deep indigo Exhibit A frame for `nutshell.png`.
- **STORY:** Cover Sheet (`14,668` frame / `1,641` sample) → `#intro` sticky SVG `stroke-dashoffset` paper-trail lifecycle → `#pick` Pull a Queue Ticket simulator with EO 2 15-day benchmark clock → `#dashboard` 6-group completion-tracker grid, Sort/Go-to toolbar, scroll-stamped RQ1–RQ3 verdicts (`H0 REJECTED`, `NO LINK FOUND · p = 0.99`), interactive SVG charts, and Exhibit A (`nutshell.png` + `nutshell.pdf`) → `#methodology` always-visible audit protocol, `<details>` statistical appendices, caveats, three-audience Call to Action, Team (`WFX · Group 1`), and Sources.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-width utility masthead (`FYI · WFX Group 1`, nav links, `Print A4 Dossier` button) above a stark Dossier Cover Sheet presenting the display title (*"Who gets an answer?"*), lead paradox (*"A fast reply is not a yes"*), and the 4-column tabular scope ledger (`14,668`, `1,641`, `6`, `524`).
