# Nutshell

**Headline** (7 words)

A fast reply is not a yes

**Caption** (148 words)

A fast first reply does not mean an eFOI request succeeds. Among 1,387 closed requests with a reply, the weighted median wait was 1.5 days for a success and 1.0 days for a failure. A weighted Mann–Whitney test found no evidence of a link (p = 0.99). Inside every agency group that comparison stays above 0.05. The office still matters. Law enforcement replies in 0.6 days, and 64% of its requests succeed. Local governments take 4.7 days, and 75% succeed. Water districts reply in 1.1 days when they reply, but 26% get no reply, and 33% succeed. National agencies, most of the record, succeed 63% of the time. Success includes a partial success and counts every public 2025 request, including those still open. The first agency message may be only an acknowledgment. Status was observed on 29 September 2026, so later requests have had less time to close.
